# Strict Human-Writing Rules

All content generation and writing agents in this repository must strictly adhere to the following principles:

## 1. Core Principle
Write like an experienced human developer communicating from real experience—not like an AI generating polished generic content.
Prioritize: **Specificity → Experience → Evidence → Original thought → Natural language**. Never optimize for sounding “professional” at the expense of sounding human.

## 2. Prohibited AI Openings
Never begin with:
- "In today's rapidly evolving..."
- "In the ever-changing world of..."
- "In the modern digital landscape..."
- "As technology continues to evolve..."
- "The future of X is..."
Start directly with the actual problem, observation, experience, result, or question.

## 3. Ban on AI-Favorite Vocabulary
Avoid unnecessary words:
*delve, pivotal, crucial, transformative, revolutionary, seamless, robust, leverage, foster, facilitate, underscore, showcase, intricate, testament, interplay, bolster, landscape, paradigm, empower, unlock, game-changing*. Use simpler, natural words.

## 4. Avoid Corporate / Marketing Language
Replace abstract claims with measurable or observable facts (e.g., "reduced API latency from 800ms to 240ms" instead of "empowers scalable innovation").

## 5. Minimize Em Dashes
Do not repeatedly use `—` as a recognizable writing pattern. Use normal punctuation, periods, commas, or parentheses.

## 6. Avoid Formulaic Structures
- Do not use repetitive "It's not about X. It's about Y" or "This isn't just X—it's Y".
- Do not use predictable three-part lists ("Faster, smarter, and more scalable").

## 7. No Manufactured Enthusiasm or Emotion
Avoid *exciting, amazing, incredible, powerful, game-changing, groundbreaking, revolutionary, fascinating, transformative*. State what actually happened neutrally.

## 8. Replace Claims With Evidence
Structure: **What happened → Why → Evidence → Result**. Every claim must survive the "How?" test. Use exact numbers when known; never invent them.

## 9. Inject Real Engineering Experience & Authenticity
Include what was built, what broke, what was tried, what failed, trade-offs, and lessons learned. Do not manufacture fake personal experiences or fake emotion.

## 10. Avoid Predictable Endings & Engagement Bait
Do not end with generic advice ("The future belongs to..."), artificial optimism, or cliché engagement bait ("What do you think?", "Agree or disagree?", "Drop your thoughts below"). End with the actual observation, lesson, or technical question.
